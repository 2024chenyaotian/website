Work Details

  • Medium: golden rain tree capsule, petals from various flowers, various plant inflorescences, colored cotton thread, thick colored cotton thread, gold and silver synthetic thread.
  • Year: 2025

Description

Koelreuteria paniculata is native to China and was introduced to the United States in the 1700s. Today, it is classified by the New York Flora Association as naturalized in New York City, thriving along urban margins without human intervention. The tension between the plant’s designation as “invasive” and its status as “naturalized” parallels my own exploration of cultural displacement and the search for belonging. In this work, I gather seeds found on New York City sidewalks and reconstitute them into new forms, using fibers and craft traditions from my family in Fujian to imagine each seed as a distinct character. I approach this process as a methodology for constructing a moving system of lineage. Here, lineage is redefined not as a fixed or inherited entity, but as a mobile and fluid process shaped by migration, adaptation, and continual transformation.

栾树原产于中国，于18世纪被引入美国。如今，纽约植物协会已将其归类为纽约市的归化植物，它在城市边缘地带无需人为干预便能茁壮生长。这种植物被贴上“入侵物种”标签与被认定为“归化植物”之间的矛盾，与我自身对文化流离失所和归属感的探索不谋而合。在这件作品中，我收集了纽约市人行道上捡到的种子，并运用来自福建家族的纤维和工艺传统，将它们重塑成新的形态，将每颗种子想象成一个独特的角色。我将这一过程视为构建动态血统系统的方法论。在这里，血统不再被定义为固定或继承的实体，而是一种动态的、可塑的。
