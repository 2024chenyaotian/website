Work Details

  • Medium: orange peel, Allium bulbil, foxtail barley inflorescence, immature fruit of buttonbush, pin oak leaf, gold-colored wire.
  • Year: 2025

Description

These sculptures use discarded plant matters found in streets, parks, and supermarkets. Collected, dried, punctured, and reassembled into miniature forms, the materials shift from living tissue to urban waste to sculptural components. They keep fading and cracking, refusing permanence and fixed completion. These works examine how time and attention can redefine what has been discarded, while refusing the idea that an artwork has to be stable, preserved, or finished.

这些雕塑作品利用街头、公园和超市中废弃的植物材料。这些材料经过收集、干燥、穿孔和重新组装，最终成为微型雕塑，其形态也随之从鲜活的植物组织转变为城市垃圾，再到雕塑的组成部分。它们不断褪色、开裂，拒绝永恒和最终的完成。这些作品探讨了时间和关注如何重新定义被丢弃之物，同时否定了艺术作品必须稳定、保存或完成的观念。
