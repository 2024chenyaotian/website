Artist Statement / 艺术家陈述

My practice investigates both the concept and the social construct of lineage. Lineage in the broadest sense means family, blood line, and cultural roots. But for me, lineage is not something I inherit but create from scratch. I come from a big patriarchal family in southern China, where women like my grandmother bear no names of themselves but only husbands’ last names. Using natural materials, fiber, and found objects, I make work that comes from a deeply vulnerable yet resilient place rooted both in my own experience, and more importantly, in a history shared by many women from my culture. Through sculpture, weaving, drawing, and research, my practice aims to make the silenced and overlooked inequality visible.
